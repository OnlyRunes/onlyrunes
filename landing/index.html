OnlyRunes

A text-adventure tribute to Old School RuneScape — free to play in your browser.

Start with nothing and build your adventure. No install, no account — your progress saves automatically.

OPEN BETA
▶ Play now ★ Open source on GitHub

Currently in open beta at beta.onlyrunes.net. A stable release will live at play.onlyrunes.net.

What you can do

⚔️

Fight

Real OSRS-style combat — melee, ranged and magic, with stab/slash/crush styles, monster weaknesses, prayer and food. Take on bosses like the King Black Dragon.

🪓

Train your skills

Chop, mine, fish, cook, smith, craft, fletch, herblore, runecraft, thieve, agility, prayer and more — every level earned.

🗺️

Explore

Roam Misthalin, Asgarnia and the Kharidian Desert, plus guilds, dungeons, members areas and the Wilderness.

💰

Get rich & gear up

Bank your loot, trade on the Grand Exchange, and upgrade your equipment from bronze all the way to dragon.

📜

Quests & goals

Complete classic quests, chase achievements, and fill out your bestiary as you slay.

🖥️

Play anywhere

Runs in any modern browser on desktop or mobile — or straight in your terminal with one Python file.

OnlyRunes is free and open source. It runs entirely in your browser — nothing to download, no sign-up — and your save lives on your device. Still actively in development: feedback and ideas are welcome.
