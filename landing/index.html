OnlyRunes

It's basically Old School RuneScape, except it's a text adventure. You type things, fight stuff, train skills, and slowly drag a level-3 nobody up toward something respectable. Runs in your browser, nothing to install, saves as you go. Free and open source.

stuff you can do:
  • fight with melee, ranged or magic — weaknesses and the right weapon matter
  • train around 20 skills: mining, fishing, smithing, fletching, herblore, runecrafting…
  • wander cities, dungeons, guilds and the Wilderness
  • do quests, chase achievements, fill out a bestiary
  • earn gp on the Grand Exchange and gear up from bronze to dragon
fair warning: this is an early beta. expect rough edges, the occasional bug, and content that shifts between visits. your save lives in your browser, so type save export if you get attached. if something breaks, let me know on GitHub — it genuinely helps.
